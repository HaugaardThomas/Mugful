# Support

Something not working, a drink missing, or an idea? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/HaugaardThomas/Mugful/issues) and I'll get back to you.

## Questions

### How does Mugful work out the caffeine?

Every drink is built from its ingredients: espresso shots, brewed coffee, milk and extras. Change the size, milk or number of shots and everything is recalculated. Real caffeine varies with beans and brewing, so treat the numbers as good estimates.

### What is "in your system now"?

An estimate of the caffeine still active in your body. It uses your **caffeine half-life** from Settings: about 5 hours for most adults, roughly half if you smoke, about double on the contraceptive pill and much longer in pregnancy.

### How is the "last call for caffeine" worked out?

From a 2023 review of 24 sleep studies (Gardiner et al., *Sleep Medicine Reviews*): a normal coffee should be finished about 8.8 hours before bed, and a stronger 217 mg drink about 13.2 hours before. Mugful scales that to your usual drink and your half-life, and moves it earlier on heavy days. Set your bedtime in Settings → Goals.

### Do I need an account?

No. Mugful works fully offline. Sign in with Apple only if you want your log backed up and synced between devices.

### How do I log water?

Tap **Add water** on the Today screen and pick a glass size or type any amount in ml.

### How do I delete my account and data?

Settings → Account → **Delete account**. Apple asks you to confirm, then your account, your backed-up data and Mugful's access to your Apple ID are removed. See the [privacy policy](../privacy/) for details.

### A drink is missing

[Open an issue](https://github.com/HaugaardThomas/Mugful/issues) with the drink's name and, if you know it, the size and where you buy it.
