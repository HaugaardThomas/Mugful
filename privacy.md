# Privacy policy

<p class="muted">Effective 21 October 2026</p>

Mugful is a caffeine and water tracker made by Thomas Haugaard, an independent developer. This policy explains what the app stores, where, and what you can do about it. The short version: **your data is yours, it isn't sold or shared, and there's no tracking.**

## Without an account

You can use Mugful without signing in. Everything you log — drinks, the time you had them, saved drinks, favourites and your settings (caffeine limit, water goal, caffeine half-life and bedtime) — is stored **only on your iPhone**. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes the data.

## With an account (optional)

If you choose **Sign in with Apple** to back up and sync your data, we store:

- **Your Apple account identifier and email address.** Apple lets you hide your email; we then only receive a private relay address.
- **What you log in the app:** drinks and when you had them, the estimated caffeine, calories, sugar and water, your saved drinks, favourites and settings.

This is stored in a database run by our hosting provider, [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy), which processes it on our behalf. Only you can read your data: every row is locked to your account.

We use this data **only** to back up your log and sync it between your devices.

## What we don't do

- No analytics, no advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
- We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
- Mugful doesn't read or write Apple Health data.

## Health information

Caffeine, sleep and nutrition figures in Mugful are **estimates** based on typical recipes and published research. They're a guide, not medical advice. If you're pregnant, have a medical condition or take medication that affects caffeine, ask a doctor.

## Deleting your data

- **Delete your account** in the app: Settings → Account → *Delete account*. This permanently deletes your account and everything backed up to it, revokes Mugful's access to your Apple ID, and removes the data from your phone. Data is removed from our live database immediately; any copies in our provider's routine backups expire automatically.
- **Clear all data** in Settings removes your log from the phone (and from your account if you're signed in).
- You can also stop Mugful's access at any time in iPhone Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the data we hold about you, and you can complain to your data protection authority (in Denmark, [Datatilsynet](https://www.datatilsynet.dk)). Mugful isn't aimed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/HaugaardThomas/Mugful/issues).
