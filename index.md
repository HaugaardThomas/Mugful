# Your coffee day, coloured in

Mugful is a cozy, hand-drawn caffeine and water tracker for iPhone. Log your usual coffee in one tap, see how much caffeine is still in your system, and get a gentle "last call" so it doesn't cost you sleep.

<div class="card" markdown="1">

- **One tap for your usual** coffees and glasses of water
- **Caffeine, calories, sugar and water** worked out from what's actually in the cup
- **In your system now:** an estimate from your own caffeine half-life
- **Last call for caffeine** before bed, based on sleep research
- **Works offline, no account needed.** Sign in with Apple only if you want a backup.

</div>
